Common questions.

  • Why can’t I just buy the course now?

    This isn’t a one-size-fits-all program. The form makes sure the course actually fits your goals, schedule, and stage. If it’s a good match, we’ll walk you through the next steps.

    You get the full structure, tools, and support to succeed — but you have to do the work. It’s not magic, it’s a system. If you follow it and take action, it works.

    It’s a hybrid setup. You go at your own pace, but you’re not on your own. Check-ins, support, and tools to help you apply what you’re learning — videos, worksheets, calculators, and guidance.
