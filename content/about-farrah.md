Farrah Ali began investing in real estate while raising two children as a
single working mother in the insurance industry. Over the years, she built
a portfolio that eventually let her retire from her corporate career and
teach others how to do the same.

Her work has been featured in the Wall Street Journal, Authority Magazine,
the Straight Up Chicago Investor podcast, and Money School Podcast. She is
the author of *Diaries of a Female Real Estate Investor*, a book written to
demystify investing for people who don't see themselves in the traditional
real estate world.

She trained through the International Board of Coaches and Practitioners
(IBCP) to become a certified life and success coach, integrating mindset
work with practical investment strategy. Today she splits her time between
investing, coaching, and teaching the curriculum that powers the Real
Estate & Wealth Building Institute.
